## Analysis audit

For PhD students, postdocs and clinical academics working with CPRD, Hospital Episode Statistics, death registrations, cohort or registry data. You send your analysis plan, code and results. I tell you what could change your conclusions, in order of how much it matters.

### What I check

- Cohort definition: index date, eligibility, look-back period, start and end of follow-up, and code lists.
- Time-related bias: immortal time, prevalent-user bias, and exposure misclassification from look-back windows.
- Outcomes: whether death or other events call for a competing-risks analysis, and whether a Cox or Fine-Gray model answers your question.
- Confounding and missing data: the adjustment set, how missingness is handled, and which sensitivity analyses a reviewer will ask for.
- Prediction models: sample size, calibration, validation and TRIPOD reporting.
- Reporting: the RECORD and STROBE items that reviewers check first.

### What you get

- A written report that ranks each problem by how much it could change your results, with a suggested fix.
- A 45-minute call to go through it.
- A fixed price, agreed before I start.

### What I need from you

Your protocol or analysis plan, your code, and your output tables. I do not need patient-level data. Most data licences, CPRD's included, do not allow it to be shared outside the approved study team.

## Larger pieces of work

If the audit shows more work is needed, I can take on a defined piece of it: a statistical analysis plan, an R pipeline from raw extract to analysis file, or the methods section of a response to reviewers.
