## Earlier work

Changes in blood, liver and kidney markers among clean-up workers over five years after the 2013 Rayong oil spill in Thailand (my PhD); air pollution and land-surface temperature trends; leprosy surveillance in southern Thailand; and neonatal mortality at a teaching hospital in Kumasi, Ghana.

## Methods

- Linked-record epidemiology: cohort design, phenotyping, code lists, incidence and prevalence, bias assessment.
- Repeated measures: linear mixed-effects models, generalised estimating equations, latent trajectory models.
- Time-to-event: Cox models, time-dependent covariates, competing risks, recurrent events, multistate models.
- Regression: Poisson, modified Poisson, quantile and generalised linear models.
- Prediction: logistic regression, random forests, gradient boosting (XGBoost, LightGBM) and survival machine learning, evaluated on discrimination, calibration, Brier score, temporal and external validation, subgroup performance and decision curves. SHAP for explanations.
- Software: R, with reusable pipelines documented on GitHub and demonstrated on synthetic data.
