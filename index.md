I work with CPRD primary care records linked to hospital admissions, death registrations and area deprivation. Most of my questions are about heart failure in people who already live with other long-term conditions, such as COPD.

## Current work

One study estimates the interval between a COPD diagnosis and a later heart failure diagnosis, and the factors associated with a longer gap. Another develops interpretable machine-learning models that predict falls in people with heart failure. Healthcare professionals, patients and carers helped decide how the risk, its uncertainty and the model explanations should be shown. Other projects cover respiratory infections and antibiotic prescribing in heart failure and COPD, and cardiovascular disease recorded before a cancer diagnosis.

## Methods

I design cohorts and code lists for linked records, and analyse them with Cox, competing-risks, recurrent-event and multistate models, mixed-effects models and GEE. For prediction models I report calibration and subgroup performance alongside discrimination, and validate on data the model has not seen. I write reusable analysis pipelines in R.
