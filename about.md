I am a health data scientist and epidemiologist at the Exeter Collaboration for Academic Primary Care (APEx), University of Exeter, where I have worked since December 2023. I studied mathematics at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology in Ghana, then completed an MSc in applied mathematics and a PhD in research methodology at Prince of Songkla University in Thailand.

## Career

- December 2023 to present: Postdoctoral Research Fellow, Exeter Collaboration for Academic Primary Care, University of Exeter, UK. Linked-data and machine-learning studies in heart failure, multimorbidity, falls, respiratory disease and healthcare inequalities.
- January to December 2023: Postdoctoral Research Fellow, Faculty of Medicine, Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. Statistical and machine-learning models for COVID-19 mortality and longitudinal critical-care outcomes.
- 2021 to 2022: Research Assistant, Division of Digital Innovation and Data Analytics, Faculty of Medicine, Prince of Songkla University, Thailand. Air-pollution trends and primary-care respiratory outcomes.

## Education

- PhD, Research Methodology, Prince of Songkla University, Thailand (2019 to 2023). Thesis: statistical analysis of the long-term health effects of Thailand's oil spill on clean-up workers.
- MSc, Applied Mathematics, Prince of Songkla University, Thailand (2014 to 2018). Dissertation: modelling neonatal mortality at the intensive care unit of a teaching hospital in Ghana.
- BSc (Hons), Mathematics, Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Ghana (2009 to 2013).

## Supervision

### MSc projects

- 2025: Machine learning visualisation of multiple long-term conditions in patients with heart failure.
- 2025: Exploring the relationship between furosemide dose and hospital admissions in patients with heart failure: evaluating statistical and machine learning approaches.
- 2026: Machine learning visualisation of patterns of health conditions and healthcare contacts: a study of multiple long-term condition burden, outpatient engagement, and emergency attendance in heart failure.
- 2026: Developing predictive models for hospital admissions at seasonal holidays.

### PhD research supervision

- 2025 to 2028: Continuity of care prediction models for multimorbid patients with a focus on heart failure and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
